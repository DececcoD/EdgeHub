# Cover Email - Tier 2 Firms

Template for the six Tier 2 candidates in `COUNSEL_CANDIDATES.md` (Greenberg Traurig, Dickinson Wright, Nelson Mullins, Duane Morris, Orrick, Brownstein). Same base ask as the Tier 1 email so responses stay comparable across all ten firms, but two differences worth noting:

1. These firms' cited experience is mostly **operator-side** (casinos, sportsbook operators), not affiliate/software-provider work like Tier 1 - the firm-specific paragraphs lean into their regulatory *depth* (drafting actual state regs, advising compliance committees) rather than a business-model match, since that's their real differentiator here.
2. Added one line up front about wanting a **fixed fee for the initial scope** - these are larger, likely pricier practices, and an early-stage company should set that expectation before a call, not after.

**Attachments to include with every send:**
- `COUNSEL_RFP.md` (convert to PDF or paste as the email body if a firm's intake form doesn't take attachments)
- `exhibits/Exhibit-A1-Terms.pdf`
- `exhibits/Exhibit-A2-Privacy.pdf`
- `exhibits/Exhibit-A3-Responsible-Use.pdf`

## Base template

**Subject:** Counsel inquiry - sports-betting market-intelligence startup (scope attached)

> Hi [Attorney/Firm Name],
>
> I'm the founder of EdgeHub (working name, pending trademark clearance), a read-only sports-betting market-intelligence product. We aggregate public sportsbook prices, show users the math behind price comparisons, and let them privately track their own betting decisions - we don't operate a sportsbook, accept or place wagers, or custody funds. Revenue comes from subscriptions plus affiliate referral fees when a user clicks through to a licensed sportsbook.
>
> We're looking for counsel to help us reach a responsible public launch. Initial scope: product/regulatory classification, state-availability analysis, marketing-claims review, affiliate-compliance guidance, and review of our draft Terms/Privacy/Responsible-Use pages - with a separately-scoped question on a possible future prediction-market (Kalshi/Polymarket) integration, which we understand may fall under a different regulatory regime (CFTC/derivatives) than the rest of this work.
>
> [FIRM-SPECIFIC PARAGRAPH - see below]
>
> One note up front: we're an early-stage company, so we'd want to scope the initial classification memo and state-availability matrix as a fixed fee if at all possible, rather than an open-ended hourly engagement - happy to discuss what that would look like before going further.
>
> I've attached a full RFP with our scope, qualifying questions for prospective counsel, and our current policy-page drafts for reference. Would appreciate 15-20 minutes to discuss fit and a rough scope/timeline/cost estimate for the initial deliverables.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> [Company]
> [Phone / email]
