# Cover Email - Tier 1 Firms

Template for the four Tier 1 candidates in `COUNSEL_CANDIDATES.md` (Womble Bond Dickinson, Saiber LLC, Walters Law Group, Ifrah Law). One base email, one firm-specific paragraph swapped in per firm - keeps the ask identical across firms (so responses are easy to compare) while showing each one you actually looked at their practice, not a mail-merge blast.

**Attachments to include with every send:**
- `COUNSEL_RFP.md` (convert to PDF or paste as the email body if a firm's intake form doesn't take attachments)
- `exhibits/Exhibit-A1-Terms.pdf`
- `exhibits/Exhibit-A2-Privacy.pdf`
- `exhibits/Exhibit-A3-Responsible-Use.pdf`

## Base template

**Subject:** Counsel inquiry - sports-betting market-intelligence startup (scope attached)

> Hi [Attorney/Firm Name],
>
> I'm the founder of EdgeHub (working name, pending trademark clearance), a read-only sports-betting market-intelligence product. We aggregate public sportsbook prices, show users the math behind price comparisons, and let them privately track their own betting decisions - we don't operate a sportsbook, accept or place wagers, or custody funds. Revenue comes from subscriptions plus affiliate referral fees when a user clicks through to a licensed sportsbook.
>
> We're looking for counsel to help us reach a responsible public launch. Initial scope: product/regulatory classification, state-availability analysis, marketing-claims review, affiliate-compliance guidance, and review of our draft Terms/Privacy/Responsible-Use pages - with a separately-scoped question on a possible future prediction-market (Kalshi/Polymarket) integration, which we understand may fall under a different regulatory regime (CFTC/derivatives) than the rest of this work.
