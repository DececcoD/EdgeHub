Template for the five prediction-market-focused candidates in `COUNSEL_CANDIDATES.md` (Stinson LLP, Holland & Knight, Lowenstein Sandler, Norton Rose Fulbright, Sidley Austin).

**Timing check before sending any of these:** `COUNSEL_CANDIDATES.md` explicitly recommends *not* contacting this tier yet - the Kalshi/Polymarket integration is a gated Phase 2 roadmap item, not a launch blocker for the core product. This file exists so you're not starting from zero later, not as a signal to send it alongside Tier 1/2. The one exception is Sidley Austin, covered separately below, since they could plausibly take the whole engagement (core product + this question) rather than just this narrow piece.

This is also a **narrower ask than the Tier 1/2 emails** - these firms don't need our Terms/Privacy/Responsible-Use drafts (nothing there touches event contracts), so there's no RFP attachment for the standard version. It's framed as an exploratory question, not an engagement request, because that's honestly what it is at this stage.
